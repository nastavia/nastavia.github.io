About the initiative

Standards that remain open to implementation

Nastavia Voluntary Standards are developed to address practical challenges emerging at the intersection of technology, governance, data, and institutional responsibility.

NAVS documents establish shared terminology, reference structures, principles, and implementation conventions. Unless expressly stated otherwise, they do not prescribe particular vendors, products, programming languages, infrastructure platforms, or deployment models.

The standards are offered for public use and continued discussion. Their voluntary nature is intended to encourage adoption, experimentation, interoperability, and gradual improvement across organizational and technological boundaries.
